1 Executive Summary & Vision

Kitonga Farm Villas is a flagship luxury eco-hospitality resort and commercial agro-enterprise. The platform fuses high-end eco-villa accommodation booking with real-time farm operations, point-of-sale commerce, agro-tour reservations, and financial accounting into a unified web ecosystem.

🌿 Guest Experience & Bookings

Explore villa sanctuaries, reserve tours, and order farm harvest with zero friction and instant receipting.

🛒 Counter POS & Real-Time Stock

Fast cashier checkout for milk, mtindi, yogurt, honey, and eggs with automated warehouse stock decrements.

2 Architectural Framework & Stack

  • Backend Core: Laravel 12.x running on PHP 8.2+ with Strict Types and Eloquent ORM.
  • Frontend Single Page Application (SPA): Vue 3 (Composition API) powered by Inertia.js v2 protocol.
  • Asset Compilation: Vite 7.x with dynamic code-splitting and asset minification.
  • Styling: Tailwind CSS with custom editorial brand tokens (`#14231C`, `#FAF8F5`, `#C98A3E`).
  • Database Engine: MySQL 8.0+ (`kitonga_villas_db`) featuring 41 relational tables with B-Tree indexing.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Spatie Laravel-Permission module.

3 Core Modules & Functionality

1. Villa Booking & Accommodation Engine

Dynamic availability matrix, double-booking prevention through atomic concurrency locks, automated invoice generation, and seasonal rate calculation.

2. POS Counter Terminal

Live touch interface, SKU/barcode fast searching, multi-gateway tender (Cash, M-Pesa, Card, Room Charge), change calculation, and 80mm/58mm thermal receipt printing.

3. Farm Produce & Dairy Inventory

Catalog tracking for all 12 authentic farm products (Mtindi 5L/3L, Fresh Milk 5L/3L, Yogurt 1L/0.5L, Eggs, Forest Honey, Mangoes, Papaws, Pineapples, Vegetables) with low-stock warnings.

4. Financial Ledger & Expense Tracking

Automated gross revenue tracking, operational expenditure logging by category, and instant net operating profit calculation.

5 Database Schema Architecture

The system runs on the relational database `kitonga_villas_db` with 41 tables:

• accommodation_types (Villa Categories)
• accommodation_units (Individual Villa Rooms)
• bookings (Guest Reservations & Dates)
• payments (Financial Tender Records)
• products (Dairy & Farm Stock Items)
• sales (POS Transaction Headers)
• sale_items (Line Items for Counter Sales)
• farm_tours (Experiences & Tours)
